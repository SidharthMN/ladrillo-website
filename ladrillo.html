The Hybrid Turnkey Model

Why Architects & Developers Partner With Us

Buying materials directly from quarries creates logistics chaos and quality discrepancies; hiring independent masons risks poor facade alignment. Ladrillo consolidates both into a single accountable partnership.

Direct-Tier Sourcing

We leverage bulk commercial purchasing relationships with granite quarries, clay brick factories, and tile plants. You receive wholesale rates without dealing with manufacturer minimum order barriers or freight complexities.

Certified Installation Crews

We do not simply dump stones at your site. Our specialized installation division executes the work using chemical adhesives, mechanical stainless-steel cramps, expansion joints, and laser levels for perfect alignment.

Single-Point Accountability

No pointing fingers between material vendors and installation labor. From crate inspection and moisture testing to post-installation silicon sealant and protective coats, we own the finish 100%.

Field Craftsmanship

The 4-Step Turnkey Execution Flow

How we eliminate material damage, misalignments, and contractor delays.

01

Quarry & Factory Curation

Our team visits the source factory, conducts dry-batch checks for color tone continuity, and verifies thickness tolerances before crate packing.

02

Substructure & Surface Prep

Laser leveling the substrate masonry. We install stainless steel mechanical clamps, expansion slots, or structural framing needed for wind loads.

03

Precision Installation

Laying by certified stone/brick masons. Consistent spacers, adhesive cavity elimination, and continuous vertical plumb line monitoring across the entire facade height.

04

Grouting, Sealant & Handover

Application of UV-resistant silicone movement joints, hydrophobic water-repellent silane sealers to prevent efflorescence, and final joint punch listing.
